    Universidad Zamorano · Europe Delegation 2026

    A century
    in a decade

    The world is living through its fastest change in history: a new technological era —digitalization, data, automation, robotics, biotechnology and connectivity— accelerated by artificial intelligence.

    The starting point

    It is not a single technology.
    It is a new era.

    Data, automation, biotechnology and connectivity are redefining every industry —from space to the field. And artificial intelligence is what accelerates them all.

    01
    Where we are today

    In just a few years, data, automation, biotechnology and connectivity —powered by AI— became the new infrastructure of every industry.

    How we got here

    From the computer to AI: half a century of acceleration

    1971–81

    The personal computer

    The microprocessor brings computing to offices and homes.

    1971: first microprocessor — 2,300 transistors
    Today: chips with 100B+ transistors · 2B+ PCs in use
    1991

    Internet

    The World Wide Web connects the planet.

    1995: 16 million online (0.4% of the world)
    Today: 6 billion — 3 of every 4 people
    2007

    The smartphone

    Internet in your pocket: data around the clock.

    2007: the iPhone is born
    Today: 5.8 billion users · 7B+ mobile lines
    2012

    Machines learn

    Deep learning takes off (AlexNet).

    2012: AI begins learning from millions of examples
    Image recognition: error 26% → 3% (better than a human)
    2022

    Generative AI

    AI answers, interprets and creates text, images and code.

    Nov 2022: ChatGPT arrives
    100 million users in 2 months · today 900 million/week
    We are here2024–26

    Agentic AI

    The leap: it no longer just answers — it uses tools, decides and carries out tasks on its own.

    From a chat that replies… to an agent that acts
    40% of enterprise apps will have agents in 2026 (up from <5%)
    ×90The world now creates about 90 times more information per year than in 2010 — every photo, sensor, purchase and message adds up, and the pace keeps rising.
    Every 6 monthsThe computing power used by AI doubles: advancing twice as fast as chips did throughout the entire computing era.
    All that information is only worth it if it turns into action. That is why AI stopped merely answering: today it decides and acts with tools to turn data into results.

    Sources: ITU / DataReportal (internet and mobile, 2025); Statista (PCs and transistors); Stanford AI Index (computer vision); OpenAI (2022–2026); Gartner (agents, 2026); IDC / Epoch AI (data and compute).

    The speed of change

    What once took a generation now takes weeks

    75 years
    For the telephone to reach 100 million users
    16 years
    For the mobile phone to do the same
    7 years
    For the Internet (the web)
    2 months
    For ChatGPT — the fastest adoption in history

    In the 1990s, the Internet reaching 100 million users in 7 years felt dizzying. ChatGPT did it in 2 months — and today AI itself doubles its power every 6 months. The curve stopped being fast: it became vertical.

    Sources: time to 100 million users (Statista; Visual Capitalist); Epoch AI (AI compute).

    The forces driving it

    It is not one technology: it is a convergence

    1

    Data

    The base ingredient: everything digital generates information that can be used.

    2

    Automation & robotics

    Machines that carry out physical and digital tasks on their own.

    3

    Biotechnology

    Genomics and breeding that redesign life and crops.

    4

    Connectivity & compute

    Cloud, chips and satellites that connect and power everything.

    And above them all: artificial intelligence — the accelerator that multiplies the value of each one.

    Adopting isn't enough

    What sets leaders apart

    1

    Reliable data

    Your own data, complete and organized: the fuel for AI.

    2

    The right questions

    Scientific thinking: science defines what to solve; AI accelerates it.

    3

    Data and AI literacy

    Reading and deciding with data at every level — using AI and knowing how to question it.

    Talent and culture

    Mixed teams, continuous learning and a culture of experimentation.

    The challenge isn't technology, it's knowledge: demand for AI talent outstrips supply 3 : 1 (IDC), and 95% of pilots fail on implementation, not on the model (MIT, 2025).
